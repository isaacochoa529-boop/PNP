# GORBO: Be the Beast 🐒

Play: https://isaacochoa529-boop.github.io/PNP/vr-monster/

You are Gorbo, a big blue beast. Six little Nibblers run, hide and slam hazard doors on you.
Catch them all before the 2 minute timer runs out.

- 🥽 Quest: push the floor and walls with your paws to move (arm swing). Grip/trigger near a Nibbler grabs it (hold it 1 second, or throw it into a wall). A/X roar, B/Y fits the floor to your paws, beat your chest for rage mode.
- 🖥️ PC: WASD, mouse, Shift, Space, click grab/smash, right click throw, Q roar, R drum.
- 📱 Phone: on-screen stick and buttons.

Rounds: vote a map in the lobby (3 pads) → 6 second head start while you're caged → 120 second hunt.
Sonar shows every Nibbler through the walls every 35 seconds. Your darkness makes nearby lights flicker out.

Maps: Rusty Factory, Yellow Maze, Frost Caves. All art, sounds and code are original (made in this file).
