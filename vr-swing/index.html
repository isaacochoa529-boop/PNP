Knuckle Grove

You're a Thumpling 🐾 a fuzzy little critter with big paws and zero legs. Slap the ground and shove it to run, leap and climb all over the grove!

🎮 Play with friends

_ _ _ _
Make a room, then tell friends the 4 numbers. Then press Enter VR!
  • Run: put your paws on the ground and push it behind you.
  • Jump: shove down hard, then let go.
  • Climb: slap a wall or tree and pull down.
  • Fit the ground to you: stand up, let your arms hang, press A or X.
  • Grab stuff: squeeze the grip button near a ball, pinecone or star, then throw it!
  • Turn: right stick. Respawn: B or Y button.
  • Desktop: WASD, mouse, Space jump, Shift run, E grab/throw, V see yourself, R respawn.
  • Play with friends: make a room or type a code up there, or poke the computer next to the spawn stump 💻 (name, colors, player ID too).
  • Grab all 12 glow berries 🫐 as fast as you can!
  • Slicing Yard: walk through the glowing portal by the spawn stump. Pull the red-hot sword from the stone and slice cubes like butter ⚔️🔥 Slow swings carve curvy cuts, SUPER fast ones are anime slices. Hold a cube in your other paw and slice it: you keep your half. There's a MEGA cube too! Green button = more cubes, red = reset. Desktop: E grabs, click swings, right-click = anime slash.
