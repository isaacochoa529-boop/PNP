Bouncy Room 🏀

Put on your Quest and see your real room. Throw balls and watch them bounce off your floor, walls, bed, couch and tables!

To see your real room, open this page on your Quest 👓
Trigger = throw a new ball 🏀
Grip = grab a ball (point at a far ball to pull it to you 🧲)
A / X = change ball type   B / Y = show room scan
Press the thumbstick = clear all balls 🧹
On a PC: click to throw, drag to look, WASD to walk
